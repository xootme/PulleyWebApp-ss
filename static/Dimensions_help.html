⚙ Dimensions and Warnings

Under the preview, a collapsible Dimensions table lists the pulley's figures: pitch, outside and root diameters, groove depth, pitch line differential, belt and face width (with the minimum the belt's standard asks for), a spline's minor and major diameters with the sample shaft's, the retaining ring, its counterbore and the washer, and — with flanges in 3D Mode — the flange's outside diameter, how far it reaches past the OD, and the minimum flange height. A figure that falls short of its minimum is shown red; one with no published figure behind it is marked ≈ (hover it for why).

Above the table, warnings say where a published rule is broken, each naming its source:

  • Face width — the pulley narrower than ISO 13050 (HTD, GT 8/14M, STD 8/14M, RPP), ISO 5294 (MXL–XXH) or Gates (GT 2/3/5M) ask for the belt, flanged or unflanged. T, AT and STD 2/3/5M have no published figure here and aren't checked.
  • Flange height — the flange reaching less far past the OD than ISO 13050 Annex D (belt tooth height + pitch differential), ISO 5294 or Gates Table 22.
  • Flange angle — outside ISO's 8–25° (it's built at the nearest limit).
  • Flange overlap — on a spoked pulley a flange stops at the spoke rim, so it sits on the pulley only as far as the rim reaches in from the bottom of the grooves (the Rim Depth). It must reach in at least the larger of 3 mm and the minimum flange height above — as much support inside the groove as the flange stands out past the OD. This is a CCT rule, not a standard's (no standard gives an inward overlap), so it is marked ≈; Auto-fix widens the spokes' Rim Depth. Without spokes the flange runs in to the hub or bore and is never short.
  • Spline reach — a spline's major diameter within 1 mm of the tooth root or the hub OD.
  • Ring counterbore — within 1 mm of the hub OD (on the top face), the spoke hub or the tooth root.

Auto-fix, beside the warnings, applies the settings that clear the ones a setting can: clearance height, flange rim radius, flange angle — and for a spline the part can't hold (1 mm of wall round its reach and the ring's counterbore), a bigger Hub OD, up to the tooth root less 1 mm a side; failing that, the largest smaller spline of the same kind (an ISO 14 size, or fewer involute teeth) that fits.

A new design's Clearance Height already makes the face the standard's minimum width, and follows the profile, belt width and flanges until you type a value of your own.

Belt Height

Belt Height (mm)
Axial thickness of both pulleys and the belt body in the 3D preview. Set to match your belt's nominal width, then add 0.5–1 mm so the belt does not rub on any flange or hub step.

Belt Height is shared by both pulleys — it is set once in the drive bar and applies to the entire assembly. Common belt widths: 6 mm, 9 mm, 10 mm, 15 mm.
Belt Height affects the 3D preview and all STL downloads — change it and the preview updates automatically.

3D Preview

Viewer Controls
Click the 3D tab in the preview panel to open the interactive Three.js drive viewer. The full belt drive — both pulleys and the belt body — renders in real time and updates automatically whenever any setting changes. All geometry is centred at the shared origin so both pulleys and the belt remain in frame after every update.

Orbit: left-click and drag.   Zoom: scroll wheel.   Pan: right-click and drag (or two-finger drag on trackpad).
What is Shown
The drive preview renders three parts in a single scene:
  • Pulley 1 (left) and Pulley 2 (right) in blue — including any hub boss and retention features.
  • Belt in dark grey/black, wrapping both pulleys with correct arc of contact on each side.

Pulleys are phase-rotated so their tooth grooves mesh precisely with the belt teeth — exactly as they would on a physical drive.
The preview geometry is exactly what the STL downloads produce — if the drive looks right in the viewer, the parts are correct.

Hub (per pulley)

Each pulley panel has its own Hub section. The hub settings in the Pulley 1 and Pulley 2 panels are completely independent — open the ⓘ help button on each pulley panel for full Hub documentation for that pulley.

A brief summary: check the Hub checkbox to add a cylindrical boss on top of the toothed section. Choose Retention Method (Set Screw std., Set Screw captured nut, or D-Shaft), Hub OD, and Hub Height. Hub geometry is shown in the drive preview and included in that pulley's STL download.

Download — STL

Three STL files are available for a two-pulley drive:
Pulley 1 STL
Toothed body + hub for Pulley 1 only. Download button in the Pulley 1 panel.
Pulley 2 STL
Toothed body + hub for Pulley 2 only. Download button in the Pulley 2 panel.
Belt STL
The belt loop solid — useful for assembly visualisation in CAD. Download from the Belt drop-down in the Pulley 1 panel.
Each file is a standalone binary STL in millimetres, ready to open in a slicer or import into CAD.
Print the two pulleys separately — they may have different bore sizes and hub configurations. The Belt STL is for visualisation; don't try to print it as a functional belt.

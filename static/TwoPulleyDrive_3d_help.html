⚙ 3D Features Guide

Belt Height (mm)

Axial thickness of both pulleys and the belt body in the 3D preview. Set to match your belt's nominal width, then add 0.5–1 mm so the belt does not rub on any flange or hub step.

Belt Height is shared by both pulleys — it is set once in the drive bar and applies to the entire assembly. Common belt widths: 6 mm, 9 mm, 10 mm, 15 mm.

Clearance Height (mm)

Extra axial height added to the pulley body beyond the belt height. Provides a small shoulder on each face so the belt sits inside the pulley rather than flush with the top face.

Default: Belt Height ÷ 20 (e.g. 0.5 mm for a 10 mm belt). Set to 0 to match belt height exactly.

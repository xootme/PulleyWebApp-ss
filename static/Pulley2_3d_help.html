When Two Pulley Drive is enabled, the 3D tab shows both pulleys and the belt together, phase-aligned so tooth grooves mesh precisely with belt teeth.

To inspect Pulley 2 in isolation, download its STL and open it in a slicer.

Spokes

Spokes
Enable the Spokes section to hollow out the pulley body with radial spokes. Reduces print time and material on large-diameter pulleys.

Spoke Count — number of spokes (2–10).
Hub OD — outer diameter of the hub boss at the spoke root.
Rim Depth — radial thickness of the solid outer rim ring.
Spoke Width — tangential width of each spoke at mid-span.
Tip / Base Fillet — corner radii at rim ring (tip) and hub boss (base).
Spoke Height — axial height of the spoke web. Max = belt height.

Flanges

Flanges
Enable the Flanges section to add belt-retention plates on the top and bottom faces. Two types are available:

Metal — thin plate with a bent-up outer rim; downloaded as separate upper and lower STL files.
3D Print — solid wedge profile; the bottom flange is merged into the pulley body. The top flange is optionally a separate part with gluing nubs.

Click the ℹ button next to the Flanges heading for full parameter details.
When flanges are enabled, a Download Flanges button appears in the Download card.

Download — STL

Download Pulley 2 STL
Binary STL of the complete 3D solid for Pulley 2 — toothed body plus any hub boss, retention features, spokes, and (for 3D-print flanges) the integrated bottom flange. Bore subtracted as a through-hole. Exported in millimetres.

Pulley 1 and Pulley 2 each have their own STL download so you can slice and print them separately with different settings (different orientations, layer heights, infill, etc.).

Flanges

Flanges are annular plates fitted to the faces of a pulley to prevent the belt from riding off axially. Check the checkbox next to Flanges to enable them. Each pulley has its own independent flange configuration.

Flange Type

Select the type with the 3D Print checkbox:

TypeDescriptionBest for
Metal (default) Thin flat plate that bends upward at the outer rim. Matches stamped or laser-cut sheet metal. Always generated as two separate files — upper and lower. Metal pulleys; standard industrial flanges
3D Print Solid wedge cross-section. The bottom flange is permanently merged into the pulley body. The top flange can optionally be a separate part for post-assembly gluing. Plastic pulleys; custom print-in-place designs

Inner Rim Rule

The flange plate extends inward only as far as the innermost applicable boundary:

  • Spokes active — inner face of the solid rim ring = tooth OD radius − Rim Depth. The flange covers only the rim ring; it does not span the spoke web or hub boss area.
  • No spokes, hub present — hub OD.
  • No spokes, no hub — bore diameter.
For the 3D-print bottom flange the combined solid (flange + pulley body) spans from flange OD to bore, but the flange plate geometry itself still stops at the inner rim boundary.

Shared Settings

  • Flange Angle — Angle of the angled section from horizontal (8–25°). Steeper angles provide better belt retention; shallower angles are easier to assemble. 15° matches most commercial flanges.
  • Rim Radius — Horizontal distance the angled section extends beyond the tooth OD (mm). Typically 2–5 mm.

Metal Flange Settings

  • Plate Thickness — Material stock thickness (mm). The outer rim of the plate is bent upward to create the flange angle. Typical: 0.5–1.0 mm thin sheet; 1.0–3.0 mm heavier plate.
  • Bend Radius — Horizontal distance from the tooth OD to the end of the bend arc (mm). Set to 0 for automatic (1.5 × plate thickness). A tighter bend is stiffer; too tight can crack thin sheet metal.
Metal flanges are always two separate parts. Download each independently from the Download Flanges menu — Upper Flange and Lower Flange.

3D Print Flange Settings

  • Flange Height — Height of the vertical lip at the outer rim (mm). The angled section rises from the tooth OD; the lip adds a vertical wall at the outer edge. Typical: 1.0–2.5 mm.
  • Top Flange as Separate Part (default: on) — When checked, the top flange is exported as a separate STL and shown 20 mm above the pulley in the 3D preview. When unchecked, the top flange is merged into the pulley body as one combined solid.

Gluing Nubs (3D Print, separate top flange only)

Nubs are small cylindrical pins on the underside of the top flange that seat into matching sockets in the pulley face, centering the flange and increasing bonding strength.

  • Number of Nubs — Equally spaced around the nub centre circle. If adjacent nubs overlap they are merged automatically. 3–6 is typical.
  • Nub Diameter — Diameter of each cylindrical pin and its matching socket (mm).
  • Nub Height — Depth of the socket cut into the pulley face (mm). The physical pin on the flange is shorter by the Socket Fit Allowance, leaving a small gap at the socket base when fully seated. Min: 1 mm. Max: one-third of belt height.
  • Socket Fit Allowance — Clearance subtracted from both the nub diameter and nub height (but not from the socket dimensions), so the pin slides freely into the socket. Default 0.2 mm. Increase for looser fit; decrease for a tighter press fit.
Nub centres lie on a circle at radius = groove-bottom radius − min(tooth height, 3 mm) − nub radius. If a nub extends inward past the inner rim boundary (spoke or hub), it is clipped at that boundary.

Hub Intersection Warning

A warning appears when the hub OD encroaches into the flange zone:

  • Spokes active and the hub OD extends inward past the spokes' inner rim, or
  • No spokes and the hub OD encroaches within 10 mm of the groove-bottom circle.
⚠ Hub intersects the flange zone.

Flange cuts hub (default: on) — the flange geometry trims the hub solid so there is no overlap. When unchecked, the hub solid trims the flange geometry instead.

Downloading Flanges

When Flanges are enabled, the Download Flanges button appears in the Download card. Open it to choose:

  • Upper Flange STL — Top-face flange (3D print: separate disc when "Top flange as separate part" is on; metal: upper plate).
  • Lower Flange STL — Bottom-face flange (3D print: base ring geometry only; metal: lower plate).
  • STEP — Available for metal flanges; solid-of-revolution body suitable for CAD, simulation, or CAM.

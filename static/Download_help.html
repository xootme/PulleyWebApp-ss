⚙ Download

⬇ Download (at the bottom of Pulley 1's panel) opens one window for the whole design, both pulleys included:

  • Parts — every part the design has, ticked: Pulley 1, Pulley 2 and the Belt in a two-pulley drive, the Whole drive drawing (DXF only), a pulley's flanges in 3D Mode when its top flange is printed as a separate part (STL only), and with a splined bore its sample splined shaft and splined washer (no STEP). Untick what you don't need.
  • Formats, one section per tier:
    • CAD shape — STEP (3D Mode): the true solid, at nominal size, for your CAD program.
    • 3D printing shape — STL (3D Mode): with the 3D Print Compensation applied; open it in your slicer (PrusaSlicer, Cura, Bambu Studio …) and print. The 3D view shows exactly what the STL will be. The file names say what is in them: GT-2M-30T.stl is the pulley with its flanges printed on it (or none); with the top flange printed as a separate part the pulley is GT-2M-30T-1flange.stl and the flange GT-2M-30T-flange-only.stl. With Add Print Supports the pulley comes twice, as is and as GT-2M-30T-w-supports.stl: ribs under the top flange and, on a spoked pulley, a breakaway grid under the hub and spokes, which stand a flange height above the bed.
    • 2D drawings — SVG and DXF: 1:1 in millimetres with true arcs, for laser, waterjet, CNC or a CAD sketch (Fusion 360: Insert → Insert SVG / DXF). The belt's DXF has separate BELT_BACK and BELT_TEETH layers.
  • Everything ticked comes as one zip. When tokens are on, the button shows what the ticks cost: one charge for the whole design at the highest tier ticked (STEP 4 · STL 3 · 2D 2 tokens), and downloading the same design again within 24 hours is free.

Gen. Rim Layer (2D, with spokes) — adds the toothed rim ring alone as its own 2D file.

Import Design

The 📂 Import button (toolbar, next to Reset) restores a previously exported design. Every SVG, DXF, STEP, and STL file downloaded from this tool contains embedded design parameters. Clicking Import, then selecting one of those files, reads the parameters and repopulates all form fields exactly as they were when the file was exported.

Only files exported from this tool contain embedded parameters. Files from other CAD programs will show an error.

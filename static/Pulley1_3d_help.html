Belt Height

Belt Height (mm)
Axial thickness of the toothed pulley body — how tall the pulley is along the shaft axis. This is the extrusion depth of the toothed section. Set to match the width of your belt. Common belt widths: 6 mm, 9 mm, 10 mm, 15 mm. Add 0.5–1 mm over the nominal belt width to avoid the belt rubbing on flanges or hub steps.

When a hub is enabled, hub height is added on top of the belt height — total part height = Belt Height + Hub Height.
Belt Height affects both the 3D preview and all STL downloads — change it and the preview updates automatically.

Hub

Enabling the Hub
Checkbox in the Hub section header. When checked, a cylindrical boss is built on top of the toothed section and included in the 3D preview and STL export. When unchecked, no hub geometry is generated.
The Hub section is only visible when Feature Build — 3D is active.
Hub OD (mm)
Outer diameter of the hub boss. May be narrower or wider than the pulley OD — no restriction. Narrower = hub boss recessed inside the tooth rim. Wider = hub flange overhanging the teeth.
Hub Height (mm)
Axial height of the hub boss above the toothed section. The bore runs through the full combined height (Belt Height + Hub Height). For captured nut retention, the height is auto-raised if the nut pocket requires more space than the entered value.
Retention — Set Screw (std.)
Radial screw hole(s) drilled through the full hub diameter — the screw can be driven from either side with a hex key. 1 screw = single perpendicular hole. 2 screws = holes placed 90° apart for more secure grip. Select screw size M3–M10 to set the hole diameter.
Retention — Set Screw (captured nut)
A stronger arrangement. A standard ISO hex nut is captured in a pocket that opens from the top face of the hub. The nut drops in from above; two vertical flat faces locate against the pocket walls to prevent rotation. The nut sits against the shaft bore. The screw enters from the hub OD, passes through the hub wall, threads into the nut, and contacts the shaft.

Screw entry is a one-sided radial clearance hole — no exit hole on the opposite wall. 2 screws are placed 180° apart (directly opposite).

Auto hub height: if the entered hub height is shorter than the nut requires, it is raised automatically. Auto oblong lobe: if the hub OD does not provide at least two nut-thicknesses of wall material outside the nut, the hub grows a rounded lobe in the screw direction.

Select screw size first — nut pocket dimensions are calculated automatically from the ISO standard for that size.
Captured nut retention is stronger than a plain set screw and can be retightened after printing without stripping the plastic bore wall.
Retention — D-Shaft
Adds a flat chord cut to the bore for a D-profile shaft. Enter the flat depth — distance from the bore wall to the flat. Measure your actual shaft; typical value 0.5 mm.

3D Preview

Viewer
Click the 3D tab in the preview panel to open the interactive Three.js viewer. The pulley — including hub boss and all retention features — renders in real time and updates automatically as settings change.

Orbit: left-click and drag.   Zoom: scroll wheel.   Pan: right-click and drag (or two-finger drag on trackpad).

Model is centred at origin and fits the view automatically.
The preview geometry is exactly what the STL download produces — if it looks right in the viewer, the STL is correct.

Download — STL

Download Pulley STL
Binary STL of the complete 3D pulley solid — toothed body plus any hub boss and retention features. Bore subtracted as a through-hole. Exported in millimetres. Open in your slicer (PrusaSlicer, Cura, Bambu Studio, etc.) and print directly — no CAD import required.

⚙ Pulley 1 — 3D Guide

3D Preview

Click the 3D tab in the preview panel to open the interactive Three.js viewer. The pulley — including hub boss and all retention features — renders in real time and updates automatically as settings change.

  • Orbit: left-click and drag
  • Zoom: scroll wheel
  • Pan: right-click and drag (or two-finger drag on trackpad)
The preview geometry is exactly what the STL download produces — if it looks right in the viewer, the STL is correct.

Spokes

Enable the Spokes section to hollow out the pulley body between the rim ring and the hub boss, replacing solid material with evenly spaced radial spokes. This reduces print time and material usage on large-diameter pulleys.

Click the ℹ button next to the Spokes heading for full parameter details.

The spoke voids also appear in the 2D SVG and DXF exports as cut profiles for laser or waterjet cutting. Use Gen. Rim Layer to export just the toothed rim ring as a separate 2D file.

Flanges

Enable the Flanges section to add annular plates on the top and bottom faces of the pulley that prevent the belt from riding off axially.

  • Metal — thin plate with a bent-up outer rim; downloaded as separate upper and lower STL files.
  • 3D Print — solid wedge profile; the bottom flange is merged into the pulley body. The top flange is optionally a separate part with gluing nubs.

Click the ℹ button next to the Flanges heading for full parameter details.

When flanges are enabled, a Download Flanges button appears in the Download card for separate upper- and lower-flange STL files.

Download — STL

Download Pulley STL — Binary STL of the complete 3D pulley solid — toothed body plus any hub boss, retention features, spokes, and (for 3D-print flanges) the integrated bottom flange. Bore subtracted as a through-hole. Exported in millimetres. Open in your slicer (PrusaSlicer, Cura, Bambu Studio, etc.) and print directly.

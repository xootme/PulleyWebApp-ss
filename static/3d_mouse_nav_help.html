Snap View Buttons

The snap bar above the 3D view jumps the camera to a preset orientation instantly. Use these to check tooth alignment, hub geometry, or belt wrap from a known viewpoint.

After snapping to a view you can still orbit, zoom, and pan freely from that position.

What Is Shown

The drive preview renders three parts in a single scene:

  • Pulley 1 (left) and Pulley 2 (right) — including any hub boss and retention features.
  • Belt in dark grey, wrapping both pulleys with correct arc of contact on each side.

Pulleys are phase-rotated so their tooth grooves mesh precisely with the belt teeth — exactly as they would on a physical drive.

The preview geometry is exactly what the STL downloads produce — if the drive looks right in the viewer, the parts are correct.

Welcome to PulleyApp

Fusion 360 edition — launched directly from the Fusion 360 sidebar.

What is PulleyApp?

PulleyApp generates precision timing-pulley profiles for HTD, GT, T, AT, RPP, STD, and Imperial belt standards. Export 2D drawings (SVG, DXF) and 3D models (STEP, STL) ready for CNC machining or 3D printing.

PulleyApp runs locally on your PC for fast, responsive performance and is launched from the CheapCAD Tools panel in the Fusion 360 toolbar.

Getting Started

  1. Open Fusion 360 and click the CheapCAD Tools panel in the toolbar, then click Open PulleyApp.
  2. PulleyApp opens in your default browser at http://127.0.0.1:5154. Keep the taskbar icon visible while using it.
  3. Configure your pulley parameters, then click Download to export SVG, DXF, STEP, or STL files.
  4. Import downloaded files directly into Fusion 360 using Insert › Import.

What is PulleyApp?

PulleyApp generates precision timing-pulley profiles for HTD, GT, T, AT, RPP, STD, and Imperial belt standards. Export 2D drawings (SVG, DXF) and 3D models (STEP, STL) ready for CNC machining or 3D printing.

This standalone edition runs entirely on your PC for fast, responsive performance. It is not integrated into CAD software — files are exported and imported manually.

Getting Started

  1. A Start Menu shortcut will be created so you can launch PulleyApp any time from the Windows Start Menu.
  2. Enter your licence key when prompted. Your key was emailed to you after purchase — check your order confirmation from cheapcadtools.com.
  3. PulleyApp opens in your default browser at http://127.0.0.1:5154. Keep the PulleyApp window in your taskbar while using it.
  4. Configure your pulley parameters, then click Download to export SVG, DXF, STEP, or STL files.

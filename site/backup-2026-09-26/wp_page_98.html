CheapCAD Tools — CAD Plug-Ins

Full CAD software packages are great. You can build almost anything with them. However, they are a general-purpose tool and building a specialized part takes time.

CheapCAD Tools plugins give you a full-screen interface dedicated to one specialized task. This simplifies your CAD process to make the part you need fast.

How the Plugins Work

Web InterfaceThe design panel looks identical to the web demo — same controls, same 3D preview — Its user interface is dedicated to one task.
Runs LocallyThe tool runs on your PC, so it responds instantly with no internet latency.
One-Click ImportDownloads for DXF or STEP files are automatically imported into your CAD workspace.
Purchase Inside your CAD software.Subscriptions are sold and managed within the CAD software's own marketplace — no separate account required.
